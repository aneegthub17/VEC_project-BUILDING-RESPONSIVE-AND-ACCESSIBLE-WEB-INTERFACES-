Recipe 1: Mushroom Manchurian

Ingredients:

  • 200g mushrooms, quartered
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 green chilies, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • Oil for frying
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions:

  1. Mix cornflour, flour, salt, pepper, and water to make a batter.
  2. Coat mushrooms in batter and deep fry until crispy.
  3. In a pan, sauté onion and chilies, add soy sauce, ketchup, and fried mushrooms.
  4. Toss well and serve hot.

Recipe 2: Veg Hot and Sour Soup

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1/2 cup chopped carrots
  • 1/2 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with water
  • 4 cups vegetable stock
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions:

  1. Boil stock with vegetables for 5 minutes.
  2. Add soy sauce, vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  3. Stir in the cornflour slurry to thicken.
  4. Serve hot with a sprinkle of spring onions.

Recipe 3: Paneer Chilli

Ingredients:

  • 200g paneer cubes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 2 green chilies, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp cornflour slurry
  • Oil, salt, and pepper

Instructions:

  1. Fry paneer cubes till golden and keep aside.
  2. Sauté onions, capsicum, and chilies.
  3. Add sauces, paneer, and cornflour slurry. Toss until coated.
  4. Serve hot as a starter or side dish.

Recipe 4: Veg Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, beans, potato)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 2 cloves, 1 cinnamon stick, 2 cardamoms
  • 2 tbsp curd
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves

Instructions:

  1. Sauté spices in oil, add onions and vegetables.
  2. Add curd, biryani masala, and salt.
  3. Layer partially cooked rice over vegetables and cook covered.
  4. Garnish with mint and coriander before serving.

Recipe 5: Butter Naan

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Warm water as needed
  • Butter for brushing
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Mix flour, baking powder, sugar, salt, yogurt, and water to make soft dough.
  2. Let it rest for 1 hour.
  3. Roll and cook on a tawa, brushing with butter after cooking.
  4. Serve hot with curries.
