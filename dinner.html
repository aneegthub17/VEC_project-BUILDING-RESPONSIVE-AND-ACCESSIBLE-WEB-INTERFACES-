    • Palak Paratha

    Ingredients:

    • 2 cups low-carb high-protein flour
    • 1½ cups spinach (palak)
    • 2–3 green chilies
    • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
    • 1 tsp carom seeds (ajwain)
    • Salt to taste
    • 1 cup water
    • 1 tbsp coconut oil

    Instructions:

    1. In a blender, add spinach, green chilies, cumin seeds, and salt. Grind into a fine paste.
    2. Mix the flour with the paste and knead into a dough. Make small dough balls.
    3. Place the dough ball between two parchment sheets and roll into a thin circle.
    4. Heat a tawa over medium flame and cook the paratha on both sides with a little ghee.
    5. Serve hot with chutney.

    Coconut Roti

    Ingredients:

    • 1 cup coconut powder
    • 2 tbsp psyllium husk
    • Pinch of salt
    • 1 tbsp curd
    • 2 tbsp buckwheat flour

    Instructions:

    1. Mix all ingredients using lukewarm water and rest for 30 minutes.
    2. Shape dough into small balls. Roll each between parchment sheets.
    3. Use a round lid to cut into perfect circles.
    4. Cook on a hot tawa for 2–3 minutes each side, with a sprinkle of ghee.
    5. Serve hot.

    Garlic Naan

    Ingredients:

    • 2 tbsp curd
    • 2 tsp coconut oil
    • ½ tsp xanthan gum
    • 1 tbsp psyllium husk
    • 6 tbsp coconut flour
    • ½ tsp baking soda
    • 2 garlic cloves, minced
    • ¼ tsp gingelly seeds
    • ½ cup warm water
    • Salt to taste

    Instructions:

    1. Mix all ingredients and knead into a soft dough. Let it rest for 20 minutes.
    2. Divide into two balls and roll into ovals.
    3. Sprinkle garlic and gingelly seeds on top.
    4. Cook on medium heat with coconut oil until golden brown. Serve warm.

    Cauliflower Methi Rice

    Ingredients:

    • 200g cauliflower (grated)
    • 75g fenugreek leaves (methi)
    • 1½ tsp ghee
    • ½ tomato, chopped
    • ½ onion, ground to paste
    • ½ tsp ginger-garlic paste
    • 1 green chili, chopped
    • ¼ tsp salt
    • ¼ cup water
    • 1/8 tsp turmeric
    • ½ tsp red chili powder
    • ½ tsp cumin powder
    • ½ tsp coriander powder
    • 1 cardamom
    • 1 bay leaf
    • 2 cloves

    Instructions:

    1. Boil cauliflower for 2 minutes, then drain and squeeze out water.
    2. Sauté spices in ghee, add onion paste and cook till brown.
    3. Add tomato, spices, and water. Then methi leaves, cook till soft.
    4. Add cauliflower rice, mix well, cook covered for 3 minutes. Serve warm.

    Coconut & Cauliflower Rice

    Ingredients:

    • ½ cup shredded coconut
    • ½ cup cauliflower (grated)
    • ¼ tsp mustard seeds
    • 2 tbsp coconut oil
    • 5 almonds, chopped
    • 1 dried red chili
    • 1/8 tsp hing (asafoetida)
    • Salt to taste

    Instructions:

    1. Heat oil, add mustard seeds until they pop.
    2. Add red chili, hing, curry leaves, coconut, almonds.
    3. Add cauliflower rice and salt. Mix well.
    4. Serve warm with dal or sambhar.
