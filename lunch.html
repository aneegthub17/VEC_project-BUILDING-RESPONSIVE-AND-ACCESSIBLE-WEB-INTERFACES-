Vegetable Stir Fry with Rice

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (broccoli, carrots, bell peppers)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce (low sodium)
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan, sauté garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add mixed vegetables and stir-fry for 5-7 minutes.
  3. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper, and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Serve hot with cooked brown rice.

Chickpea Salad

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup boiled chickpeas
  • 1 cucumber, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, and onion.
  2. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Toss well.
  3. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve chilled.

Vegetable Pulao

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, beans)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups water

Instructions:

  1. Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds and sauté until they splutter.
  2. Add onion and garlic, sauté until golden.
  3. Add vegetables and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Add washed rice, water, and salt.
  5. Cook covered on low heat until rice is done. Serve hot.

Dal Tadka

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup yellow moong dal
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cook dal with water, turmeric, and salt until soft.
  2. Heat ghee, add cumin seeds, garlic, onion, and tomato, and sauté well.
  3. Pour this tempering over the cooked dal and mix.
  4. Serve hot with rice or roti.

Quinoa Salad

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1/2 cup diced cucumber
  • 1/2 cup diced bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup corn
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Mix quinoa, cucumber, bell peppers, and corn in a bowl.
  2. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice, add salt and pepper.
  3. Toss gently and serve fresh.
