Beverage 2: Cold Coffee

Ingredients:

  • Milk
  • Instant coffee powder
  • Sugar
  • Ice cubes
  • Vanilla ice cream (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Blend milk, coffee powder, sugar, and ice cubes.
  2. Add vanilla ice cream if desired.
  3. Serve chilled.

Beverage 3: Chocolate Banana Smoothie

Ingredients:

  • Banana
  • Cocoa powder
  • Milk
  • Honey

Instructions:

  1. Blend banana, cocoa, milk, and honey until smooth.
  2. Serve immediately.

Beverage 4: Mint Lemon Cooler

Ingredients:

  • Mint leaves
  • Lemon juice
  • Honey
  • Cold water
  • Ice cubes

Instructions:

  1. Blend mint leaves with a little water.
  2. Mix lemon juice, honey, and mint extract with cold water.
  3. Add ice cubes and serve chilled.

Beverage 5: Hot Chocolate

Ingredients:

  • Milk
  • Cocoa powder
  • Sugar
  • Chocolate chips (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Heat milk and whisk in cocoa powder and sugar.
  2. Add chocolate chips if desired.
  3. Serve hot.
