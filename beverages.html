    Coconut Milk Coffee

    Ingredients:

    • 1/2 tsp instant coffee powder
    • 1/2 tsp stevia
    • 1/2 cup water
    • 1/2 cup grated coconut

    Instructions:

    1. Grind the coconut in a mixer grinder with 2 tbsp of Hot water.
    2. Squeeze out the coconut milk with the help of strainer and pour it in a cup and keep aside.
    3. Add stevia and brewed coffee powder to the coconut milk.
    4. Serve hot.

    Avocado Strawberry Smoothie

    Ingredients:

    • 150g strawberries
    • 1/2 avocado
    • 1/8 tsp lemon juice
    • 4 tbsp greek Yogurt (Unflavoured)
    • 200g skimmed milk

    Instructions:

    1. Add all the ingredients in a blender and whizz until smooth.
    2. If the consistency is too thick, add a little water.
    3. If you like your drink sweet, add stevia.
    4. The smoothie is ready to serve.

    Berries Spinach Smoothie

    Ingredients:

    • 1 cup spinach
    • 1/2 cup strawberries
    • 1/4 cup blueberries
    • 1/4 cup raspberries
    • 1 cup almond/coconut milk (unsweetened)

    Instructions:

    1. Add all ingredients into a blender and blend until it comes to smooth.
    2. Pour into a glass and enjoy.
    3. For a thicker smoothie, add more spinach or berries.
    4. For a thinner smoothie, add more almond/coconut milk.
    5. For a sweeter smoothie, add a little honey or stevia.
