Avocado Toast

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices whole grain bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Chili flakes (optional)
  • 1/4 tsp Lemon juice

Instructions:

  1. Toast the bread slices until golden brown.
  2. Mash the avocado in a bowl, add lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread the avocado mix onto the toast.
  4. Sprinkle chili flakes if desired and serve immediately.

Vegetable Upma

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup semolina (rava)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil

Instructions:

  1. Dry roast the semolina and set aside.
  2. Heat oil, add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  3. Add onions, chili, carrots, and peas, sauté for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Add water and salt, bring to a boil.
  5. Slowly add the roasted semolina, stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
  6. Cook until water is absorbed. Serve hot.

Smoothie Bowl

Ingredients:

  • 1 banana
  • 1/2 cup frozen berries
  • 1/2 cup yogurt (or plant-based yogurt)
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • Granola and fresh fruits for topping

Instructions:

  1. Blend banana, berries, yogurt, and chia seeds until smooth.
  2. Pour into a bowl.
  3. Top with granola, fresh fruits, and more chia seeds if desired.
  4. Serve immediately.

Moong Dal Chilla

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup moong dal (soaked and blended)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for cooking

Instructions:

  1. Mix moong dal paste with onion, chili, turmeric, and salt.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan, spread a ladleful of batter like a pancake.
  3. Drizzle a few drops of oil around and cook both sides till golden brown.
  4. Serve hot with chutney or yogurt.

Oats Porridge

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk or almond milk
  • 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • Fruits (banana, berries) for topping
  • 1 tbsp chopped nuts

Instructions:

  1. In a saucepan, combine oats and milk. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally.
  2. Once thickened, remove from heat and stir in honey or syrup.
  3. Top with fruits and nuts.
  4. Serve warm.
